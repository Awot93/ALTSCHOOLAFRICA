Biography

I trained as a chemist. I hold a B.Sc. from the University of Ilorin and an M.Sc. from the University of Ibadan, and the lab taught me to work methodically and to trust evidence over guesses.

I now work in technical support and operations in a banking and fintech environment. My days involve payment switching, third-party bank API integrations, debugging SQL, troubleshooting applications, and helping plan server migrations. I work with Docker, within the limits of my server access.

Outside my job, I am building AGROGLOBAL VENTURES, an export business that sources palm oil and honey for retail and bulk buyers abroad. I also handle administrative and communications work for a community organisation.

Why I joined the program

Every day I see how much depends on software working correctly, and I am usually the person who finds out when it does not. I want to be the one who builds it, not only the one who reports the fault.

I know the basics of Java, Python and C#, up to control statements, but I have not yet built a real project. I needed structure, deadlines, and people to learn alongside. I also need a program that fits around a full-time job, and AltSchool does.
